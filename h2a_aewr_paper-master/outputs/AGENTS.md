# Retained-output instructions

- Files here are generated manuscript products with an owning script. Do not repair values, labels, notes, or formatting directly unless the task is explicitly a prose-only retained-output correction and the divergence is documented.
- Before replacing an output, record the input artifact versions, runner, environment locks, model/specification IDs, and validation evidence.
- Preserve diagnostic and null/negative results. Do not delete a table or figure because it is inconvenient for the preferred interpretation.
- Large or transient outputs belong in ignored paths; only intentionally retained products should be committed.
