# AEWR-border redesign instructions

- Treat `design.json` as the executable v6 registry. The redesign is exploratory
  and informed by v5 outcomes; never select a model by its coefficient or p-value.
- Preserve the original four geography/seven model v5 registry and its complete
  audit as labeled benchmarks. Stage04's diagnostic primary is the OLD h1 model;
  the structural redesign candidate and its sensitivities live in stage07.
- Geography may use only 2008--2010 crop/climate features, static soil, 2010
  adjacency/land area, 2007 cropland, 2008--2010 farm employment and stable
  AEWR-region membership. Both endpoints of qualifying edges retain the frozen
  7.5% cropland, 30 farm jobs/100sqmi and 90% coverage rules.
- New screened geographies apply the same support rules to EVERY member before
  disjoint assignment. Iteratively require an own-region retained path within
  one step of a surviving qualifying edge and a passing opposite-core feature
  caliper for noncore members. Preserve connectivity and actual boundary support.
- Region matching uses the declared maximum-cardinality/core-edge/lexical rule
  based only on geography. More memberships do not create independent regions.
- Retain the direct current-real-AEWR treatment, 2011--2022 policy sample,
  certifications/fixed-2011-farm-employment outcome, and county/component-year
  FE. Fixed 2008--2010 employment weights change the weighted population, not
  the outcome denominator. The sole history adjustment is baseline mean outcome
  interacted with linear time; disclose its additional trend assumption.
- Keep original pair CRV1/WCR11-FNW/Webb9999/seed201103/CV3J and raw-row
  pair-by-region CRV1 as comparisons. Dyadic inference covers either shared
  endpoint but remains a small-node sensitivity; preserve negative variances
  and failed/unbounded confidence sets. Region deletion is influence, not CV3J.
- Preserve the declared bounded size/power DGPs, one-thread resource guard,
  seeds, signed effects and MDE grids. No randomization inference without an
  assignment mechanism; no observed-effect post-hoc power or noise tuning to
  promote a favorable specification.
- Canonical 2009 AEWR is null; application means have a separate nonstatutory
  field. Stage04 alone restores `v5_aewr_ppi_comparator` for the labeled old
  diagnostic. New wage diagnostics exclude 2009; FE-only noise calibration
  retains all three 2008--2010 outcome years without requiring an observed wage.
- `data_input_design.json` owns the four fixed-denominator sensitivities and
  native/common samples. Keep their units explicit, preserve size failures, and
  never select the primary denominator by estimated effects or standard errors.
  Allocated position-days are certification capacity, not realized employment;
  capacity-weighted wage references cannot become predetermined instruments.
- Use publicly released NASS inputs; non-public NASS access/tabulations are
  unavailable by user instruction. The county analysis does not require a
  farm-level nonparticipant frame. Such a frame would be needed to estimate
  farm participation, a separate unsupported outcome. Keep public geographic
  utilization measures distinct from farm adoption probabilities.
- Validate geography, keys, role/weight/sample metadata, exact FWL/gap/deletion
  equivalence, covariance and bootstrap behavior, simulation size and signed MDE.
  Keep adverse findings. Generated reports must distinguish implementation,
  integrated validation and empirical adequacy; no automatic manuscript/welfare use.
- `statewide_design.json` is a separate, user-requested registry for the
  2025-10-02 statewide-AEWR state-border design (stages 12--13). It never
  changes the v6 registry or outputs. Its geography, treatment, outcome,
  calibration years and scenarios were frozen before any post-policy outcome
  was parsed; stage 13 must fail if a post-policy fiscal year is present and
  must not use post-period residuals for scale. Estimation is a later stage
  that requires upstream FY2025+ disclosure parsing; keep failed gates.
