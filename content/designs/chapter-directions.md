## Offered wages

The estimand is the change in continuing filer-state-occupation mean offers per
dollar of the unchanged US skill-I statutory dose, within former AEWR regions.
Baseline certified hours provide the primary weights; equal weights are
separate. R7 identifies filers, not worksite businesses. R5/R8 and disclosed
FEIN are identity sensitivities. Common-occupation calculations hold shares
fixed at baseline but condition on occupation survival in both periods.

Causal interpretation requires no differential state shocks correlated with
the dose, stable measurement and a defensible continuation comparison. These
extensions were developed after related FY2026 results were seen. Receipt
date does not establish job-order submission date; skill, worker category,
prevailing wages and multi-state itineraries remain unresolved. Exact effective
floors stay unknown. The separate four-schedule envelope combines observed
US/H-2A skill-I/II rates and agricultural minimum wages as reference values; it
does not identify the applicable legal floor or a market wage.

## Administrative interruption

Recurring filer-state-start-month cohorts are fixed using 2011 and 2012.
Exposure is the equal-case share of projected baseline processing intervals
overlapping October 1–16, 2013. All cohorts remain in the five-year panel.
Observed record absence is distinct from verified nonfiling. The primary
outcome is observed certification by the historically predicted start date;
that benchmark is not an observed hiring requirement.

The dated measurement amendment retains only canonical requested-start dates.
FY2015 lacks them, so calendar 2014/2015 outcomes and temporal placebos are
unknown, not zeros. The feasible 2011–2013 contrast uses cohort and
state-season-year effects with state deletion jackknife inference. Baseline
filing in both years is selected by construction, so its lead is mechanical.
Temporal comparisons do not turn one national shutdown into many independent
shocks. Conditional timing cannot identify a pure delay effect.

Offered wages do not identify realized earnings, employment, monopsony or

identification and reliable measurement beyond a statistically significant
coefficient.
