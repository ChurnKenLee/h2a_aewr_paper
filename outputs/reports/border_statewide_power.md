# Statewide-AEWR border design: declared power

Generated by `13_statewide_power.R`; registry `border_statewide_aewr_v1` (frozen 2026-10-18); seed 201103; 999 repetitions per DGP.

## What this measures

The 2025-10-02 rule replaced regional FLS AEWRs with statewide rates. Within a former FLS region, adjacent states had identical AEWRs before 2026 and different ones afterwards. The simulation fixes the actual state-border geography and the actual 2026/2027 rates, injects signed effects, and draws errors from the v6 county/state/pair/spatial processes, scaled by pre-period outcome residuals after county and pair-year fixed effects. No post-policy outcome is read; this is design-stage power, not post-hoc power. A combination passes when every shared-dependence DGP (state, state-plus-pair, spatial) passes the size gate, power at both signs of the threshold is at least 0.80, and the largest size-valid MDE is at most the threshold.

## Outcome `certifications_fiscal_year`

Units: certified positions per fixed-2011 farm worker per nominal $1/hour (0.01 equals 10 per 1,000); fiscal year of decision. Declared primary threshold: 0.01.

## Limits

Simulated size and power are conditional on assumed error processes; they do not establish that state AEWR gaps are exogenous. The rule also changed rates nationally, added a state housing adjustment and coincided with other 2025 policy changes; state-specific concurrent shocks would bias the design and are not represented here. Skill-level assignment is an employer choice, so the primary treatment is an intent-to-treat statutory floor. FY2026 includes cases decided under prior rates. The wage outcome requires post-period offers in the same counties. Estimation requires the upstream producers to parse the 2025 and later disclosure files.
