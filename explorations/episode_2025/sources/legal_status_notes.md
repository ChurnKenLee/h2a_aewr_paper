## What this means for the designs

- **FY2026 Q1–Q3 is unaffected.** The rule was in force, not enjoined (a
  preliminary injunction was denied in May 2026, per secondary sources), and
  backpay cannot reach work before 2026-09-02.
- **From 2026-08-26 employers knew the rule was unlawful, and from 2026-09-02
  work is exposed to possible backpay.** The wage an employer expected to pay
  for later work is not simply the statutory rate.
- **The earlier record needs annotating.** Amendment 1 of the 2025-rule
  registry records the user's statement of 2026-11-02 that challenges to the
  rule failed. For FY2026 Q1–Q3 the rates did apply throughout, which is all
  that registry relies on; the statement is wrong about the litigation's
  outcome.
- **No replacement rates exist yet.** The third event stays undeclared.
