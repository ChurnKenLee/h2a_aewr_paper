### Diagnosis and Prioritized Decision

Prior designs fail on **spatial concentration** (3.78–13.3 effective clusters; Florida’s 42.6% share) or **weak residual doses** (cents in C1/A2; 0.0071 log SD on 2.2% of hours in A3; 142 contractor filers). Viable designs need sub-state or filer variation in core field work.

**Decision:** Run **(3) ACA cutoffs** first (zero external merges) with **(1) SWA prevailing wages** (largest wage shock), then **(2) county enforcement** (widest cluster base). Hold **(4)** and **(5)** as demand benchmarks; gate **(6) state overtime** on max state share (<35%), effective clusters, and pre-trends.

### Six New 2008–2025 Designs

1. **SWA Prevailing-Wage Switches (Wage Floor).** *Assignment unit:* Sub-state area by crop and year. *Source:* OFLC Wage Library and H-2A disclosures. *Falsification:* Same-county crops below AEWR; lead hours. *Power screen:* Log-floor SD ≥0.03 after county-year and crop FEs; ≥30 effective areas; ≥10% of field hours. *First test:* Wage pass-through and hours around survey “no-finding” reversions.

2. **County Interior Enforcement (Unauthorized Supply).** *Assignment unit:* County-year (2008–13 Secure Communities; 2008–25 287(g)) times 2007 farm labor share. *Source:* ICE rosters, TRAC removals, 2007 Ag Census, OFLC worksites. *Falsification:* Pre-trends; mechanized grain counties (<5% labor share). *Power screen:* ≥100 effective counties after state-year FEs; state weight <20%; removal F>20. *First test:* State-year FE event study of county requests.

3. **ACA Mandate and Seasonal Safe Harbor (Benefit Cost).** *Assignment unit:* Filer-contract cell near 50/100 FTEs and the 6-month cutoff (2015–16). *Source:* IRS Section 4980H rules and OFLC disclosures. *Falsification:* 2011–13 changes; placebo cutoffs (120 days; 35 workers). *Power screen:* ≥500 baseline filers near 35–120 workers; hours MDE ≤5%. *First test:* Bunching shifts at ≤180 days and <50 FTEs plus filer hours.

4. **Pre-Window vs. In-Window Weather Shocks (Harvest Demand).** *Assignment unit:* County season by shock timing around the 45–75 day filing deadline. *Source:* NOAA nClimGrid, USDA RMA indemnities, OFLC dates. *Falsification:* Post-contract weather; greenhouse orders. *Power screen:* ≥150 effective counties; RMA loss F>30. *First test:* Compare hours and withdrawals for freezes 65–110 vs. 5–40 days pre-start.

5. **2018–19 Retaliatory Tariffs Net of MFP (Output Price).** *Assignment unit:* County-year 2012 crop shares times HS tariffs net of MFP. *Source:* USITC schedules, USDA FSA rates, 2012 Ag Census, OFLC hours. *Falsification:* 2014–17 pre-trends; untariffed produce. *Power screen:* ≥25 effective crop shocks; residual SD ≥0.02 log revenue after state-year FEs. *First test:* Regress county hours on net tariff exposure (2015–20).

6. **State Agricultural Overtime Phase-Ins (Marginal Hour Cost).** *Assignment unit:* Border county-crop or California firm-size cell (≤25 vs. ≥26 workers) times pre-2018 >40-hour share. *Source:* State statutes and OFLC disclosures. *Falsification:* Baseline ≤40-hour orders; untreated same-AEWR border counties. *Power screen:* ≥8 effective border clusters; stability dropping California or Washington. *First test:* Weekly-hour bunching at statutory caps and headcount substitution.
