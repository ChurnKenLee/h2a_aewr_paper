## 3. Structural Diagnosis: Why Cached Local Data Hit an Irreducible Power Wall

Recall the clustered MDE identity:
$$\text{MDE}_{\text{cost}} \;\propto\; \frac{\sigma_\varepsilon}{\sqrt{G_{\text{eff}}} \cdot \text{SD}(\tilde{X}) \cdot \pi}$$

Across all six unexplored levers, our Phase 1 investigation proves empirically that **every single factor in this fraction faces a hard structural ceiling on cached public data**:

1. **Numerator ($\sigma_\varepsilon$) cannot be compressed by observables, lags, factors, or firm FE:**
   - **Exogenous covariates (`R2b`):** County-level predetermined `CDL × Year` (4 crop groups) and 6 `NOAA` climate-anomaly PCs (`85.0%` climate variance) barely move `MS_SY / MS_county` (`2.85 → 2.80–2.92` on `2011–2025`; `3.02 → 3.02` and `1.84 → 1.86` on `2025`), shifting MDEs by `-1.2%` to `+6.6%`. Unobserved state-year agricultural demand shocks are orthogonal to broad crop-acreage shares and annual climate PCs.
   - **Pre-period averaging, SDID, ANCOVA, and IFE (`R2c`):** County and state H-2A hours behave nearly like a **random walk** with strong permanent drift. Replacing `t-1` with a 3-year pre-average (`+37%` worse) or SDID time weights (`+14%` worse) inflates MDE. On `2011–2025` bite, dynamic ANCOVA (`+ ln(1 + H_{t-1})`, FE-only persistence $\hat{\rho} = 0.2695$) lowers short-run MDE (`0.488`) only at the cost of Nickell bias and a *worse* long-run steady-state MDE (`0.488 / 0.7305 = 0.668 > 0.625`). Meanwhile, Bai (2009) iterative rank-1 IFE (`r=1`) with exact delete-one-region SVD refit CV3J absorbs `44.8%` of residual variance — **but simultaneously absorbs `49%` of `SD(X̃)` (`0.1496 → 0.0765`)**, leaving size-valid 2-SD MDE at `0.639` (`> 0.60`), while `r=2` **breaks empirical size (`0.105 [0.070, 0.155] > 0.075`)** by overfitting `G = 17` regions!
   - **Firm-level & within-multi-state-filer absorption (`R3`):** Fixing balanced direct-grower business cohorts (`R3a`, `20,980` 1-yr / `14,512` 2-yr businesses with explicit `Y=0` exits) preserves `SY = 2.44` (PPML) and `23.93` (equal-weighted DHS) because business-level exit (`14.9%–22.2%/yr`) and lumpiness dominate, worsening MDE to `0.780–1.175` (PPML) and `2.031–3.630` (DHS). Absorbing `filer × FY FE` among multi-state filers (`R3b`) **does** completely erase the state-year shared shock (`SY = 0.83, p = 0.739`) and boosts $G_{\text{eff}}$ to `21.02` — yet multi-state contractor reallocation across states is so lumpy (`0 ↔ 100%` contract shifts) that MDE explodes to **`1.688`** (`46st all`) and **`3.195`** (`46st direct`).
2. **Pass-through ($\pi$) & competing floors (`R1`, `R5`) do not create high-power within-state bite:**
   - **State minimum-wage truncation (`R1a`/`R1b`):** Only **7 of 46** multi-state Track 1 states (`CT, ME, MO, NY, OR, RI, WA`, max gap `$1.85`) have `mw_ag > AEWR_US1` on April 1, 2026. Truncating the statutory floor leaves offered-wage pass-through virtually unchanged (`R7` $\hat{\pi} = 0.620$ vs `0.623`; county $\hat{\pi} = 0.655$ vs `0.671`) and trims blind MDE by only `2.4%` (`1.016 → 0.992`). Isolating the pure truncation gap (`R1b`) fails the first stage (`R7 F = 1.73`, county `F = 0.34`, hours MDE `5.96`).
   - **Sub-state tiers (`R1c`):** On April 1, 2026, **only 1 state (`NY`, `3.29%` of US hours, $G_{\text{eff}} = 1.00$)** has within-state sub-state ag minimum-wage variation above `AEWR_US1` (OR standard `$15.05` and nonurban `$14.05` both sit below OR's `$15.20` AEWR until July 1, 2026).
   - **Local-wage crossover bite (`R5`):** Across **14 within-state (`State FE`) offered-wage first-stage specifications** spanning `OEWS Big-Six`, `ACS p25`, and `QWI NAICS 111` (crossover gap `$`, binary crossover × dose, market-truncated cut; filer-county-SOC `h0`-weighted and county equal-weighted), **0 of 14 passed** $F \ge 10$ and $|\Delta\hat{\pi}| \ge 0.25$ ([r5_first_stage_audit.csv](file:///home/ken/Dropbox/projects/H-2A_Paper/explorations/bite_power_rescue/r5_first_stage_audit.csv)). The highest `F` was `8.18` (`OEWS` gap, $\hat{\pi} = 0.117 [0.041]$), the largest $|\hat{\pi}|$ was `0.130` (`ACS p25` gap, $F = 4.20$), and `QWI 111` was wrong-signed (`-0.053, F = 2.03`). Even after a `-$3.74/hr` statutory cut, local county wages generate almost zero differential H-2A wage pass-through.
3. **Effective clusters ($G_{\text{eff}}$) and stacked episodes (`R6`) cannot rescue `policy_2025`:**
   - Stacking the `2009 → 2010` OES-to-FLS reversal with `2025 → 2026` (`R6`) fails all three gates: (i) the `2009–10` offered-wage first stage under `County + Former-Region × Period FE` fails $F \ge 10$ (`eq-wt` $\hat{\pi} = 0.172 [0.133], F = 1.67$; `pos-wt` $\hat{\pi} = 0.276 [0.148], F = 3.50$); (ii) because `2009–10` H-2A volume (`77,181` positions) is `3.89×` smaller than `2025` (`300,249`), raw `2009–10` contributes only **`6.4%` (dollar) / `13.5%` (log)** of total Fisher information — triggering the explicit `< 15%` kill rule; and (iii) even under equal-event normalization, stacked positions MDE is **`0.620`** (`raw 0.685`), failing to beat `policy_2025`'s single-event `0.52`.
4. **Structural cost-share scaling (`R4`) creates a catch-22 between 2-SD MDE and $\varepsilon_{\text{cost}}$:**
   - All `2025` `State × FY FE` duration/labor-share specs (`R4a`/`R4b`) either suffer severe size distortion when omitting `E` main trends (`county dur` size = **`0.331`**, `business dur` size = **`0.088`**) or lose variance when controlling for `E` main trends (`MDE_2SD = 1.503–2.554`).
   - On `2011–2025`, `Census 2007 >=150d` (`2-SD = 0.645–0.689`) and `BEA farm labor share` (`0.847–0.916`) miss the `0.60` gate. Only `2008–2010` H-2A contract-duration `s_wage(F=$2500)` (`R4a`) cleared Phase 1 on standardized 2-SD units (`0.523–0.534`), **but** because hours-weighted $\text{SD}(s_{\text{wage}}) = 0.0588$ (`2-SD = 0.118`), its structural $\varepsilon_{\text{cost}}$ MDE is `~4.0–4.6` — and it fails Phase 2 on duration mean reversion (Section 5).

### 5.1 Why `R4a` (`P1`/`P2`, 2011–2025 Duration-Amortization Bite) Is KILLED

Despite clean `2008–2010` pre-trends (`p = 0.51–0.94`), baseline covariate balance (`|r| <= 0.038, p >= 0.155` across crop, farm, and demographic covariates), and tight `2-SD` MDEs (`0.517`/`0.551`), the adversarial referee audit uncovered **three fatal structural failures**:

1. **Fatal Flaw 1 — Mechanical Mean Reversion in Baseline Contract Duration:**
   - Certified **positions** move in the *wrong (positive)* direction: **`+0.1950 [CV3J 0.1441], p = 0.195`** (`C+RY`) and **`+0.2370 [0.1453], p = 0.122`** (`C+SY`).
   - Exact paired-CV3J **implied contract duration (`theta_hours - theta_positions`)** plunges by **`-0.3067 [0.0731], 95% CI = [-0.462, -0.152], p = 0.0007`** (`C+RY`) and **`-0.3348 [0.0743], 95% CI = [-0.492, -0.177], p = 0.0004`** (`C+SY`)!
   - The event-study path ([phase2_r4a_event_study.csv](file:///home/ken/Dropbox/projects/H-2A_Paper/explorations/bite_power_rescue/phase2_r4a_event_study.csv)) confirms pure post-baseline mean reversion: hours drop linearly from `2012 (+0.011)` to `2016 (-0.143)` and then sit **completely flat through 2025 (`-0.162, -0.168, -0.159, -0.132, -0.126, -0.128, -0.131, -0.141, -0.146`)** even as nominal AEWR surged `+40%` between 2018 and 2025, while workers *rose* (`+0.020` in 2016 → `+0.102` in 2021–2023). Moreover, replacing the concave amortization formula `s_wage(F=$2500)` with raw linear `mean_weeks_0810` produces an even stronger negative slope (`-0.145 [0.075], p = 0.072`), whereas restricting to post-2020 direct growers (after mean reversion had run its course) yields dead zero (`-0.030 [0.089], p = 0.742`).
2. **Fatal Flaw 2 — `96.5%` Reliance on National `E_dur × Year` Trends:**
   - Absorbing `E_dur × Year FE` (`R4a_1a_ppml_plus_Edur_x_Yr`) retains only **`3.47%` (PPML-wt) / `2.74%` (unwt)** of `1b` variance, **flips the sign from `-0.112` to `+0.3905 [0.4471], p = 0.395`**, and inflates `t(16)` MDE to `1.334` (`2.8×`: `1.252`).
3. **Fatal Flaw 3 — Uninformative Structural Cost-Share Elasticity ($\varepsilon_{\text{cost}}$):**
   - Regressing directly on raw `s_wage_2500 × ln(AEWR)` yields **`-1.210 [CV3J 1.436], 95% CI = [-4.25, +1.83]`** (`C+RY`, $t(16)$ MDE **`4.285`**) and **`-1.375 [1.557], 95% CI = [-4.67, +1.92]`** (`C+SY`, $t(16)$ MDE **`4.646`**). Because county cash-wage shares are compressed (`hours-wt SD = 0.0588`), even a `0.52` 2-SD differential MDE cannot bound structural $\varepsilon_{\text{cost}}$ within `±4.3`.

**Why `P3`/`P4` are clean diagnostics — yet cannot replace primary designs:**
1. **Still no structural or aggregate elasticity:** As `code/designs/bite/` and our `R5` audit demonstrate, `E_p25` has near-zero differential offered-wage pass-through (`93%–97%` of H-2A hours sit at the statutory AEWR regardless of county `wage_p25`). Thus `P3`/`P4` remain **reduced-form local-wage-gap differentials**, not aggregate labor-demand elasticity $\eta$ or cost elasticity $\varepsilon_{\text{cost}}$.
2. **`P3` (`1b-PPML`) still relies `95.37%` (PPML-wt) / `95.64%` (unwt) on national `E_p25 × Year` trends**, shows baseline rurality correlation (`ln_pop_2010` $r = -0.295, p < 0.001$; `farm_emp_share_0810` $r = +0.174, p < 0.001$ — though controlling for `+ X_pre × Year FE` leaves `P3` completely unchanged at `+0.071 [0.069]`), and exhibits a slight positive duration drift (`+0.100 [0.046], p = 0.045`). Decomposing the `0.758 → 0.434` (`2.8×`) MDE gain against `code/designs/bite/` shows that ~60% (`0.758 → 0.557`) comes from shortening the horizon to 6 years (`All 2020–2025`, less cumulative 15-year drift) and ~40% (`0.557 → 0.434`) comes from excluding H-2ALC churn.
3. **`P4` (`1a-PPML`) is driven by a single year (`2025`) and concentrated in two Southeast regions:** Dropping provisional calendar start-year `2025` (`2020–2024` strictly pre-Oct-2-2025 FLS regime) moves `P4` from `-0.299 [0.192]` to **dead zero (`-0.036 [0.223], p = 0.873`)** (whereas `FY2020–FY2025`, which excludes all `FY2026` filings, stays at `-0.251 [0.223]`, proving the shift is 2025 FLS cross-region divergence rather than Oct–Dec 2025 spillover). Furthermore, `P4` retains only `4.63%` of `1b` variance, has **`G_eff = 7.28`** with **Region 10 (`AL,GA,SC`) supplying `30.6%`** and **Region 11 (`FL`) supplying `11.6%`** (`42.2%` combined), attenuates by a third when dropping `FL` (`-0.299 → -0.202`) or top-1% counties (`-0.299 → -0.195`), and has `2×` wider SEs on certified positions (`-0.352 [0.377], p = 0.363`, `t(16)` MDE `1.124`).
4. **Genuine diagnostic contribution:** Even with those limitations, `R2a` (`2020–2025` direct growers) is the **only specification in the entire rescue investigation that survives both Phase 1 and the Phase 2 referee audit as a clean null**. In particular, `P4` (`2020–2024` `1a-PPML` = `-0.036 [-0.508, +0.436]`, `2.8×` MDE `0.624`; `2020–2025` `1a-PPML` = `-0.299 [-0.707, +0.108]`, `2.8×` MDE `0.538`) cuts `code/designs/bite/`'s `1a-PPML` MDE (`2.106`) by **`70%–74%`** without leaning on `E_p25 × Year` trends, confirming that the full-panel null is not an artifact of H-2ALC churn or national `E_p25 × Year` confounding.
