PRE-STAGE: DATABASE CONSTRUCTION (2002 & 2017 Benchmark)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Script: 0a_PRESTAGE.R

Goal: Build the complete FIPS, CZ and PR level picture of the US crop
      sector in 2002 and 2017 -- input use (land, labor, water, fertilizers
      and other inputs), cost of inputs, and crop production -- ready for
      first-stage estimation.

Input sources (read from in/<source>/):
  - Census of Agriculture (CoA), via USDA QuickStats extracts    [crop output/VCROP by subgroup,
                                                                     harvested cropland, land rents,
                                                                     payroll costs, intermediate inputs]
  - National Agricultural Workers Survey (NAWS)                 [worker-reported hourly wages, datasets constructed by Alexandra E Hill 
						  - aggregating data from the US Census of Agriculture, Farm Labor Survey, QCEW, 						  - NAWS, and H-2A programmatic data. For code to compile these data sources, 
						  - contact alihill@berkeley.edu]

  - Farm Labor Survey (FLS)                                      [hourly wages for hired workers,
                                                                     alternative to NAWS, 1997-2024]
  - Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW / BLS)        [real wages in non-farm sectors]
  - NLCD & MIrAD (satellite)                                     [cropland area]
  - USGS                                                          [water withdrawal data]
  - Fair Market Rent (HUD)                                       [housing rental costs, used for
                                                                     H-2A and non-wage cost estimation]
  - SIMPLE-G GRID/CZ database (.har files, read via HARr)         [2010 benchmark cost shares]
  - Charlton & Taylor (2016)                                      [immigrant labor supply shock:
                                                                     central estimate ~1% annual decline]
  - BLS CPI tables (hardcoded conversion factors in-script)       [inflation adjustment to 2002$/2017$]

Crosswalks (in/Crosswalk/):
  - GRID_FIPS_STATE.xlsx        SIMPLE-G grid <--> FIPS <--> State
  - FIPS2CZ_251024.xlsx         3,100+ counties --> 605 Commuting Zones (CZs)
  - CZ2PR.xlsx                  605 CZs --> 9 Farm Resource Regions (Heimlich 2000)
  - FIPS2PR_071024.xlsx         FIPS counties --> Farm Resource Regions
  - CZ_names.xlsx, GRID2PR.xlsx, GRID2FIPS2LS.xlsx, GRID2FIPS2CZ.xlsx

Processing steps (in-script section numbers):
  1.1  Read crosswalks, GRID/FIPS/CZ/PR reference tables
  1.2  Estimate 2010 benchmark input cost-shares from GRIDDATA.har / CZDATA.har
  1.3  Construct price indices (commodity groups, CPI conversion factors, fuel price)
  2.1  OUTPUT data: combine Census VCROP sub-group sales (grain/F&N/vegetable/other),
       long-term-average and quality-adjust, aggregate to FIPS, CZ, and PR (QC_CZ, QC_PR)
  2.2  LABOR data: FLS/NAWS wage series (1997-2024, CPI-adjusted), employment hours,
       hourly hiring cost by data source and shock scenario
  2.3  INTERMEDIATE INPUTS (chemicals, seed, fuel, machinery, other -- QZ/VZ)
  2.4  RESOURCES: land (harvested cropland, rental rates) and water (USGS withdrawals)
  2.5  Labor supply shocks (SS Shocks: immigrant decline scenarios, PHIL)
  2.6  Land + water resource aggregation
  2.7  TFP change calculation
  3    Assemble final estimation inputs: data_fips, data_cz, data_pr
       + 36 labor scenarios (NAWS/FLS wage source x low/med/high shock x
         ConSe/NonAg non-farm pull proxy), consolidated for Steps 1-2

  temp/0_data_fips_071326.xlsx       Observable data at FIPS level [crop output,

  temp/0_QLABOR_FIPS_071326.xlsx     Labor market outcomes at FIPS level [employment

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: FIRST-STAGE ESTIMATION OF UNKNOWN PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Script: 1_FIRST_STEP.R  (sources scripts/NM_obj_function.R, scripts/FSE_PP.R,
                          scripts/PLOT_FSE.R)

Goal:
  Simultaneously estimate, for each of the 9 Farm Resource Regions (F):
    AFL   -- % change in labor-biased partial factor productivity (PFP)
    AFZ   -- % change in intermediate input-biased PFP
    AFR   -- % change in resource-biased PFP (land & water)
    ESLZ  -- price elasticity of labor-land/water substitutability (sigma)
    ELAB  -- price elasticity of labor supply (eta)

Method:
  Constrained Nelder-Mead optimization (scripts/NM_obj_function.R, Equation 9)
  Objective: cost-share-weighted sum of squared deviations between observed
             and SIMPLE-G-CZ-simulated input use for each Production Region
  Constraints: bounds on AFL/AFZ/AFR in [5,200], ESLZ in [0.5,1.5]xmean_sig,
               ELAB in [0.1, 1.01]
  Runs: 30 independent runs per scenario x 36 scenarios (scripts/FSE_PP.R),
        each run calling GEMPACK (simplez.exe -cmf ...) per region/iteration
  Note: FSE_PP.R is long-running and is commented out by default in
        1_FIRST_STEP.R for routine reruns -- pre-computed estimates are read
        from in/fse/1_list_est.rds by PLOT_FSE.R instead. Uncomment the
        source("scripts/FSE_PP.R") line to regenerate estimates from scratch.

Input files:
  temp/0_data_cz_071326.xlsx, temp/0_data_pr_071326.xlsx,
  temp/0_data_fips_071326.xlsx, temp/0_shares2010_071326.xlsx,
  temp/0_labz_scenarios_071326.RDS, temp/0_laba_scenarios_071326.RDS  (see Pre-Stage)

Output files:
  temp/1_list_est.rds                Raw Nelder-Mead estimates (all runs/scenarios),
                                        written only when FSE_PP.R is (re-)run
  temp/PR##/PR##_pfp.xlsx            Per-region intermediate estimate files (PLOT_FSE.R)
  temp/1_pfp_est_pr.xlsx             Median + MAD parameter estimates by Region and
                                        scenario group (AFL/AFZ/AFR/ESLZ/ELAB) --
                                        primary input to Step 2
  plots/Figure6/*.png                Visualization of calibrated parameter
                                        distributions across regions/scenarios

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: HISTORICAL VALIDATION, DECOMPOSITION & COEFFICIENT IDENTIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Script: 2_SECOND_STEP.R  (sources scripts/Data_plots.R)

Goal:
  (a) Validate: confirm SIMPLE-G-CZ with Step 1 estimates reproduces observed
      2017 employment and hourly hiring costs
  (b) Decompose: attribute observed changes (2002-2017) to drivers -- expansion
      effect (Q), factor-biased productivity changes (AFL/AFZ/AFR), labor supply
      shock (PHIL_L), non-farm pull (PHIL_C), water (QW) and input price (PZ) --
      using the Harrison, Horridge & Pearson (1999) decomposition
  (c) Identify coefficients: responsiveness of employment and wages to a 1%
      change in each driver, by Farm Resource Region, with 95% CIs from a
      729-run (3^6) bounding analysis over low/median/high first-stage estimates

Method / run sequence:
  i.   Write exogenous controls and labor-supply-shock .har files to shf/
       (OBS_DATA.har, NonAg_[L/M/H].har, ConSe_[L/M/H].har, FSE-<group>M.har)
  ii.  Run GEMPACK median simulations via run.simple() -> system() calls:
         simplez.exe -cmf 2-Validation-FLS-NM3.cmf
         simplez.exe -cmf 2-Validation-NAWS-NM3.cmf
         simplez.exe -cmf 2-Validation-FLS-CM3.cmf
         simplez.exe -cmf 2-Validation-NAWS-CM3.cmf
  iii. Generate CI text files for the stochastic (SSA) bounding runs
       (temp/3_SSA_SHK_*.txt, temp/3_SSA_PARM_*.txt) -- these drive the
       external 729-combination SSA batch (run separately in GEMPACK; results
       read back from ssa/ and in/ssa/)
  iv.  Read back .sl4 solution files, build historical validation figures
       (Figure 7) and the decomposition tables (Table 4)
  v.   Read pre-computed 729-run beta coefficients (in/ssa/3_ssa_<source>-
       <phil>_beta_[pl/ql]_729.xlsx) and plot Figure 8 (coefficient estimates
       with 95% CIs, expansion-effect panel)

Input files:
  temp/0_data_cz_071326.xlsx, temp/0_data_pr_071326.xlsx,
  temp/0_data_fips_071326.xlsx, temp/0_shares2010_071326.xlsx                 (Pre-Stage)
  temp/0_labz_scenarios_071326.RDS, temp/0_laba_scenarios_071326.RDS         (Pre-Stage)
  temp/0_PHILz_071326.RDS, temp/0_PHILa_071326.RDS                           (Pre-Stage)
  temp/0_QLABOR_FIPS_071326.xlsx, temp/0_QLABOR_CZ_071326.xlsx,
  temp/0_QLABOR_PR_071326.xlsx                                                (Pre-Stage)
  temp/1_pfp_est_pr.xlsx                                                      (Step 1)
  in/ssa/3_ssa_<source>-<phil>_beta_pl_729.xlsx,
  in/ssa/3_ssa_<source>-<phil>_beta_ql_729.xlsx                               (external SSA batch)
  in/List_CZ.xlsx                                                             (CZ outlier drop list)
  2-Validation-<FLS/NAWS>-<N/C>M3.cmf                                         (GEMPACK control files)

Output files:
  shf/OBS_DATA.har, shf/NonAg_*.har, shf/ConSe_*.har, shf/FSE-*.har           GEMPACK inputs
  out/2-Validation-<source>-<phil>M3.sl4                                     GEMPACK solution files
  temp/3_SSA_SHK_<source>_<phil>.txt, temp/3_SSA_PARM_<source>_<phil>.txt    CI specs for SSA batch
  temp/3_pl_decomp_<source>-<phil>.xlsx, temp/3_ql_decomp_<source>-<phil>.xlsx  Decomposition detail
  plots/Figure7/hist_vali_lab_<sel/oth>_<source>_<phil>.png                  Observed vs. simulated
                                                                                wages & employment (2017)
  plots/Figure8/<source>_<phil>_ed.png                                       Coefficient estimates
                                                                                (wage & employment
                                                                                responsiveness) with CIs
  Table 4 (tab_pl, tab_ql, in-console; export commented out by default)      Decomposition table
